Clean Home

A small home screen for one Android TV at home. It is a personal project, not an app you can download, and it has no ads, accounts or tracking.

What it shows

  • A clock, today's weather and your next calendar event.
  • The apps installed on the TV, in the order you choose.
  • Continue watching and the rows that apps like Netflix publish for the TV home screen.
  • New videos from the YouTube channels you subscribe to, once you sign in to YouTube on the TV.

Signing in to YouTube

Clean Home asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account, only to see which channels you subscribe to. It can't post, like, comment or change anything. You can disconnect at any time from the launcher's options on the TV, or from your Google Account's connections.
