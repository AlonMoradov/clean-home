Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

Clean Home is a home screen app for a single Android TV in a private household. It is not published or sold. This page explains what it reads and what it does with it.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in to YouTube on the TV, Clean Home asks Google for one permission: read-only access to your YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly). It uses it for one thing: getting the list of channels you subscribe to, and your channel's name to confirm which account is connected. It then shows the newest public videos from those channels on the TV's home screen.

Clean Home does not read your watch history, playlists, comments, messages, email, contacts or anything else in your Google Account, and it can't change anything in it.

Where it's kept

The sign-in token Google issues, the list of subscribed channel IDs and the most recent videos are saved only on the TV, in Clean Home's private app storage. They are not sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's own (Google's sign-in service and the YouTube Data API) and YouTube's public channel feeds, which are how the videos are fetched.

Sharing

Clean Home does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It has no ads, analytics or tracking, and your data isn't used to train AI or machine-learning models. Clean Home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other things Clean Home uses

  • Weather: the town you set (for example Beer Yaakov) is looked up with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and its coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. No account is involved.
  • Calendar: upcoming events are read from the calendar the TV already syncs, using Android's calendar access on the TV. They are shown on screen and never sent anywhere.
  • App rows: rows that other apps publish to the TV (such as Netflix and Continue watching) are read from the TV itself.

Removing your data

On the TV, hold OK on the Settings button and choose Disconnect YouTube. This deletes the token, channel list and videos from the TV and tells Google to cancel the access. You can also remove Clean Home's access at any time from your Google Account's connections. Uninstalling Clean Home deletes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
